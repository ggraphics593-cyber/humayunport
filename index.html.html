FAQ

Common questions

What do I need to start a project?

Share a short brief: what you need, your brand name and colors if you have them, and any examples you like. I will guide you with the rest.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the project. Logos and thumbnails are usually quick; websites take longer. Tell me your deadline and I will confirm a timeline before starting.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes. I work with you on feedback until the design matches what you need, within the agreed scope of the project.

Which file formats will I receive?

Typically PNG and JPG for web and social, plus editable or vector source files for logos and print designs where applicable.

Are your websites mobile friendly?

Yes. Every website is built to be fully responsive, so it looks good on mobile, tablet and desktop.

How can I hire you?

Message me on WhatsApp, send an email, use the contact form, or order through my Fiverr or Upwork profile.
